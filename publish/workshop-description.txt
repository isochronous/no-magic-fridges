No more magic fridges. This mod changes what a fridge does to the temperature of its contents [b]while it is powered[/b]. An unpowered fridge is untouched.

[h2]The problem[/h2]
In the base game, an unpowered fridge is just a box: the food inside exchanges heat with the room. The moment the fridge gets power, every item inside is pulled towards 1 °C, from both directions. Warm food is cooled to 1 °C, but frozen food is [i]heated[/i] to 1 °C as well. If you keep a fridge in a freezer room so the food is deep-frozen, powering it, even for a few ticks to read its automation signal, drags your frozen food back up to 1 °C.

[h2]What power does with this mod[/h2]
A powered fridge behaves like a real one: an insulated box with a thermostat.
[list]
[*][b]Powered, contents warmer than 1 °C:[/b] the fridge cools them down towards 1 °C, as in the base game.
[*][b]Powered, contents at or below 1 °C:[/b] the fridge does nothing to them. It never heats. Deep-frozen food stays deep-frozen however often you switch the power on. The cold food is also insulated from the room the same way an insulated tile insulates (heat exchange slowed to 1/100, not stopped), so a powered fridge holds its cold far longer than an unpowered one.
[*][b]Powered, mixed contents:[/b] the items share heat with each other, in both directions. Frozen stock chills a warm newcomer fast and is warmed a little in return. Put 5 kg of 30 °C food into 95 kg at -20 °C and it all settles at -17.5 °C: the heat is conserved, not deleted.
[*][b]Thermal buffer matters.[/b] A single warm item in an empty or cold fridge is chilled about as fast as in the base game. A fridge crammed with warm food (after a long power cut, say) cools at roughly a fifth of that rate, because a small compressor is working against a big warm mass.
[*][b]Unpowered:[/b] exactly the base game. The food exchanges heat with the room at the normal rate, and nothing in this mod applies. Food taken out of a fridge also goes straight back to normal.
[/list]

Power use, the full/not-full signal (which still needs power, as in the base game), the energy-saver state and spoilage rules are unchanged.

Applies to the Refrigerator, the Mini Fridge, and any modded building that reuses the game's fridge controller.

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
Works with the base game and all DLCs, on build 744825 (U59) or newer. No dependencies. Nothing is written to your save, so it can be added or removed at any time.

Formerly published as "Fridges Only Cool". If you had that version enabled, enable "No Magic Fridges" in the Mods menu after updating: the mod's internal ID changed with the name.
